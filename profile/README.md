# Rymna Lab

Rymna Lab is building AI-powered research workflow software for active individual investors.

Our first product helps swing traders organize market information, build watchlists, review setups, and maintain a consistent research process.

We do not provide brokerage services, automated trading, personalized investment advice, or investment performance guarantees.

## Focus

- AI-assisted market research workflows
- Watchlist organization
- Pre-market and post-market summaries
- Stock research records
- Trade review support

Most repositories are private while the product is in early development.
